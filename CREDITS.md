# Credits

Every third-party asset in NIGHTWARDEN is released under **CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain)**.
Raw files are fetched by `tools/fetch_assets.py` and optimised by `tools/build-assets.mjs`
into `public/assets/` (WebP textures, max 2K; meshopt-compressed / simplified GLB models).

All characters, brands, street names, places, vehicles and logos in the game are original
and fictional. Anything not listed below (city layout, buildings, roads, the Narrows bridge,
the clock tower, lighthouse, cranes, all nine vehicle types (Halden Pip, Marisco Vela,
Corvane Strata / Strata Tourer, Oberline Ridgeback / Carrier, Tessaro Mule, Vellmoor Cabs,
Vellmar Citiline bus), bus shelters, flags, trees, street lamps, traffic signals, signage, the
sky, clouds, stars, moon, rain, lightning, UI, minimap and the NIGHTWARDEN emblem — and for
Stage 3 the Nightwarden's suit shader, armour plates, hood, cowl, half-mask, visor lenses,
crescent emblem and cloth cape, the gang members' weapons and riot shields, hangout props,
roof vents, updraft steam, combat effects and gadget effects — and for Stage 4 the three gangs
(Tidewater Crew, Ashline Syndicate, Velvet Hand) and their outfits, hats and colours, the
Vellmoor Police Department uniforms, caps, vests and helmets, the VPD Interceptor and Bastion
vehicles and their livery and lightbars, the VPD helicopter and its searchlight, spike strips,
the warehouses, the Brightwater Savings / General Hospital / VPD Precinct 1 signs, fire and
smoke, tracers, muzzle flashes and bullet marks, zip ties and the crime HUD) is
procedurally generated by this project's own code. All sound except the Kenney samples below is
synthesised at runtime with WebAudio (engine, skids, traffic, horns, sirens, rain, wind,
thunder, birds, gulls, crickets, bells, foghorn, screams, punches, grapple zips, gadgets, the
glide air rush, the ambient music, and for Stage 4 police sirens, helicopter rotors, the bank
alarm bell, gunshots, fire, spike strips and police-radio blips). Stage 4 adds no new external
assets.
